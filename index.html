RFID is a rapidly developing technology that uses radio waves to collect and transmit data. RFID is used in a variety of industries including healthcare, retail, manufacturing, transportation, and more.

Historically, RFID technology has been used in supply chain management, primarily to track goods in warehouses. Compared to barcode scanning, RFID does not require line-of-sight for readers to capture information from tags. Although RFID is being adopted in a variety of industries, including construction and transportation services, its implementation and use is sometimes a challenge.
